## Bootstrap boundary policy

The logit clips AUC at 1e-6 and 1 - 1e-6. Over 2000 child-clustered resamples of the 1966 host test segments and 156 (model, arm) score columns, the number of resample AUCs equal to 0 or 1 is 0; the smallest and largest resample AUC are 0.6128 and 0.9126. The clip is therefore never active on this host; if it were, the affected resamples would enter the percentile interval at the clipped value (not dropped), and the count would be reported.
